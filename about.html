About Orivon

Orivon is an AI-powered career development platform built to help students, graduates, and professionals grow their careers — through personalized mentorship, real guidance, and tools that actually move you forward.

Our Belief

We believe career guidance shouldn't be a privilege reserved for the well-connected or the well-funded. It should be available to anyone with the drive to grow — regardless of where they start.

  • ✓ Everyone deserves access to quality career guidance.
  • ✓ Opportunities shouldn't depend on location or financial status.
  • ✓ AI can make mentorship and career support accessible to everyone.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide personalized, AI-powered career guidance at scale — combining mentorship, interview preparation, CV reviews, and structured career planning into one platform that grows with you.

Instead of generic advice, Orivon learns from your goals and progress to give you a path that's actually built for you — not a one-size-fits-all roadmap.
