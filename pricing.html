FAQ

Common questions.

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out and we'll help.

Can I change my plan anytime? +

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your account settings. Changes to your billing take effect at the start of your next cycle.

Is there a free trial? +

Our Free plan gives you ongoing access to core features with no time limit, so you can explore Orivon before upgrading. Pro also includes a 14-day money-back guarantee if it's not the right fit.

What payment methods are accepted? +

We accept all major credit and debit cards. Enterprise customers can also pay via invoice or bank transfer — just contact sales to set that up.

Can universities purchase in bulk? +

Yes. Our Enterprise plan is built for universities, bootcamps, and organizations, with volume-based pricing, team management, and institution-wide AI Mentor access. Contact sales for a custom quote.

Is my data secure? +

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never sell your personal information. Enterprise customers can also request a dedicated data processing agreement.
